COSMODL Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

What COSMODL does

COSMODL is a private livestream recording and archiving tool operated for the account owner.

Google Drive access

When authorized, COSMODL uses Google Drive access only to upload and manage recordings requested by the account owner. COSMODL does not sell Google data or use it for advertising.

Data handling

Livestream recordings and related metadata are stored on the operator’s recording server and, when enabled, in the owner’s Google Drive. Access tokens are used only for the authorized Drive integration and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

Contact

For questions about this application or removal of its access, contact the application owner through the Google account associated with the authorization.
